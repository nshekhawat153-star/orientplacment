## 7. Our 4-Step Placement Process: Built for Speed & Precision
- **Eyebrow**: `THE ORION METHODOLOGY`
- **H2 Headline**:
  - `From Vacancy to Verified Hire in Four Rigorous Steps`
- **Steps**:
  - **Step 01: Deep Strategic Intake & Benchmarking**:
    - We conduct a 45-minute discovery session with your key stakeholders to define hard skill requirements, team dynamics, compensation structures, and long-term KPIs.
  - **Step 02: Targeted Passive Talent Outreach**:
    - Using our proprietary talent database and direct headhunting protocols, we reach out to the top 10% of industry practitioners—focusing on high performers who aren't on job boards.
  - **Step 03: 360-Degree Screening & Shortlist Presentation**:
    - Every candidate undergoes in-depth behavioral interviews, technical qualification assessments, and credential verifications. You receive a curated dossier of 3-5 top contenders.
  - **Step 04: Interview Logistics, Offer Close & Onboarding**:
    - We manage scheduling, collect candid feedback, handle delicate compensation negotiations, navigate counteroffers, and maintain contact through the 90-day onboarding window.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions (Homeowner/Hiring Manager Objections Answered)
1. **How does your fee structure work?**
   - For direct hire placements, we operate primarily on a success-based contingency model: you owe nothing until your chosen candidate signs and starts. For retained executive searches, we offer phased engagement milestones.
2. **How quickly can we expect to see candidates?**
   - For standard professional and managerial roles, you will receive your first curated shortlist within 5 to 10 business days. For urgent contract or operational roles, we can often present vetted talent within 48 hours.
3. **What happens if a candidate doesn't work out?**
   - We stand firmly behind our vetting process with a 100% Replacement Warranty (90 to 180 days depending on the agreement). If a candidate leaves or fails to meet expectations, we will find a qualified replacement at no additional charge.
4. **How do you find passive candidates who aren't applying online?**
   - Over 70% of our successful placements come from direct outbound headhunting. Our partners maintain relationships with top performers across legal, healthcare, tech, and finance who are not active on job boards.
5. **Can you handle nationwide or multi-state hiring?**
   - Yes. Orion Placement operates across all 50 states, handling both local on-site leadership and remote/hybrid specialized talent.
6. **I'm a job seeker. Do I have to pay anything?**
   - Absolutely never. Our services are 100% free for candidates. We represent you confidentially and help you negotiate the best compensation package possible.
